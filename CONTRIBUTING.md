# Contributing to vlc-skins-high-dpi

Thank you for your interest in improving VLC's usability on modern displays! This is a research-driven project, and we welcome contributions from designers, developers, and power users.

## How to Contribute

### 1. Research & Documentation

We value data as much as code. You can contribute by:

- **Measuring existing skins:** Help us document the actual pixel sizes of controls in various VLC skins.
- **Testing on different hardware:** Test our prototype skins on your specific display/scaling combination and report the results.
- **Adding to research:** Find and document touch/DPI guidelines from other media players or OS HIGs.

### 2. Skin Development

If you are creating or modifying `.vlt` skins:

- **Follow the Guidelines:** Refer to `docs/touch-guidelines.md` and `docs/high-dpi-guidelines.md`.
- **Focus on the MVP:** Our current priority is the `default-touch` skin.
- **Provide Evidence:** When submitting a PR for a UI change, include screenshots at 100%, 200%, and 300% scaling.

### 3. Issue Reporting

- Use the provided issue templates to report usability bugs or suggest new research targets.
- Be specific about your OS, display resolution, and scaling factor.

## Contribution Workflow

1. **Find an Issue:** Look for "good first issue" or research-related tasks.
2. **Fork & Branch:** Create a feature branch for your work.
3. **Implement & Document:** Make your changes and update any relevant research notes.
4. **Submit a PR:** Provide a clear description of the change and the evidence (screenshots/measurements) that justifies it.

## Code of Conduct

Be respectful and constructive. We are working toward a more accessible and usable media player for everyone.
